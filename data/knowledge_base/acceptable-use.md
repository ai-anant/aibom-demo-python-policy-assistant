# Acceptable Use and Data Handling Policy

Confidential data may only be processed with approved AI tools. Pasting customer
records, source code or credentials into unapproved chat assistants is a
reportable security incident.

AI-generated content must be reviewed by a human before it is sent to a
customer or published. The reviewer is accountable for accuracy.

Approved models are listed in the AI service catalog maintained by Security.
Requests for new models go through the AI review board, which meets biweekly.

Prompt and response logs are retained for 90 days for audit purposes, excluding
content classified as restricted.

Suspected data exposure must be reported to security@acme.example within one
hour of discovery.
