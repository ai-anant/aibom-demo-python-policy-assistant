# Remote Work and Equipment Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require a written exception from the People team and
are reviewed every twelve months.

The company provides a laptop and a USD 500 home-office stipend in the first
year of employment. Equipment remains company property and must be returned
within 14 days of offboarding.

Working from a public network requires the corporate VPN. Storing customer data
on personal devices is prohibited.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees in different
time zones should agree on overlap hours with their manager.
