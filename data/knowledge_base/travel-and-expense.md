# Travel and Expense Policy

Employees may book economy travel for flights under six hours and premium
economy for longer routes. All travel must be booked through the corporate
travel portal at least seven days in advance unless it is an emergency.

Hotel limits are USD 250 per night in tier-1 cities and USD 150 elsewhere.
Meals are reimbursed up to USD 75 per day with itemised receipts.

Expense reports are due within 30 days of the trip end date. Reports submitted
after 60 days require director approval. Corporate cards may not be used for
personal expenses, including minibar and in-flight entertainment.

Receipts above USD 25 are mandatory. Missing receipts require a signed
attestation form from the employee's manager.
